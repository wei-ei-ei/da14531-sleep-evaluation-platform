# Related Research Literature

This is an initial reading list for sleep monitoring, PPG and heart-rate variability. The papers provide background only; their results do not establish the accuracy of this development platform.

## Sleep sensing and validation

### Wearable Sleep Technology in Clinical and Research Settings

- Massimiliano de Zambotti, Nicola Cellini, Aimée Goldstone, Ian M. Colrain, Fiona C. Baker
- *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*, 2019, 51(7), 1538-1557
- DOI: [10.1249/MSS.0000000000001947](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001947)

This review covers consumer sleep trackers, validation requirements and factors such as proprietary algorithms, firmware changes and device reliability.

## PPG and accelerometer sleep staging

### Estimation of sleep stages in a healthy adult population from optical plethysmography and accelerometer signals

- Z. Beattie, Y. Oyang, A. Statan, A. Ghoreyshi, A. Pantelopoulos, A. Russell, C. Heneghan
- *Physiological Measurement*, 2017, 38(11), 1968-1979
- DOI: [10.1088/1361-6579/aa9047](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6579/aa9047)

The paper studies sleep-stage estimation using optical plethysmography and accelerometer signals, matching the main sensor modalities of this project.

## Heart-rate variability and machine learning

### Sleep stage classification from heart-rate variability using long short-term memory neural networks

- Mustafa Radha, Pedro Fonseca, Arnaud Moreau, Marco Ross, Andreas Cerny, Peter Anderer, Xi Long, Ronald M. Aarts
- *Scientific Reports*, 2019, 9, 14149
- DOI: [10.1038/s41598-019-49703-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-49703-y)

This work demonstrates an LSTM approach to sleep-stage classification from heart-rate variability and provides a reference for feature-based analysis.

## Reading cautions

- Published performance depends on sensor placement, study population and reference scoring.
- PPG-derived inter-beat intervals are not automatically interchangeable with ECG-derived intervals.
- Consumer sleep stages should be reported with validation method, sample size and limitations.
- This project must not use literature results as a substitute for its own validation.
